Epidemic spread

Not from Observable — the classic Susceptible → Infected → Recovered/Deceased model, laid out as a real contact network instead of a chart. Blue nodes are susceptible, red are contagious, green have recovered and can't be reinfected, and gray have died and no longer take part. Each tick, every infected node has a chance to pass it to each susceptible neighbor it's actually connected to (not just anyone nearby) before eventually recovering — or not.
