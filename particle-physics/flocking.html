Percolation

Each cell in the grid is independently open with probability p. A path "percolates" if open cells connect all the way from the top row to the bottom — exactly the model used for how liquid seeps through porous rock, or how a disease/fire/electrical network spans a random medium. On an infinite square lattice the odds flip from "almost never" to "almost always" astonishingly fast right around p ≈ 0.593, the critical threshold. Drag the slider to feel that phase transition, or click/drag cells to toggle them by hand.
