About Kinetic

Build it. Change it. See what happens. Kinetic exists to make real science feel exciting instead of abstract — not animations of physics, but physics; not a diagram of an atom, but one you can rotate and inspect; not a list of orbital facts, but a solar system you can actually fast-forward through and watch move. Every number behind every simulation here is a real, working formula, not a canned effect — the goal is that playing with something here should teach you at least as much as reading about it would.

Who made this

Kinetic is an independent project built by Evan Marr, a hobbyist developer and lifelong science enthusiast who kept wishing textbooks let you actually touch the thing they were describing. It's built and maintained as a free, ad-free educational resource.
